Watch on YouTube: Dr. Eric Berg · Dr. Ben Winters

What published reviews report

These are the findings of the named sources, not a verdict from this site.

  • Cochrane review, updated 2024: fluoridation may slightly reduce tooth decay in children. The benefit in newer studies is smaller than in the early studies, when fluoride toothpaste was not yet widely available.
  • Calgary vs Edmonton: Calgary stopped adding fluoride in 2011 while Edmonton kept it. About seven years later, about 65% of Calgary second-graders surveyed had tooth decay, compared with about 55% in Edmonton. Calgary voted to bring fluoride back, and it returned in June 2025.
  • NTP monograph, August 2024: moderate confidence that higher fluoride exposure, such as drinking water containing more than 1.5 mg/L, is associated with lower IQ in children. Insufficient data to determine whether the low level of 0.7 mg/L currently recommended for U.S. community water supplies has a negative effect on children's IQ.
